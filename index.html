Privacy Policy for GreenSpot

Last Updated: January 16, 2026

Quick Summary: GreenSpot collects only location data and photos you voluntarily share for environmental monitoring. We don't collect personal information, sell data, or use advertising. All environmental reports are public by design for civic accountability.

1. Overview

GreenSpot is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our environmental monitoring platform.

2. Information We Collect

Location Data

  • GPS coordinates when you register trees or report environmental issues
  • Used solely for mapping and environmental monitoring
  • Stored with reports but not linked to your personal identity

Device Information

  • Anonymous device identifier to prevent duplicate reports
  • Generated locally on your device
  • Cannot identify you personally
  • Used only to prevent spam and duplicate submissions

Photos

  • Photos you upload for tree registration and environmental issue reporting
  • Stored securely on Cloudinary CDN
  • Associated with location data but not personal identity

Environmental Data

  • Air quality readings for your location via WAQI API
  • Vegetation coverage data from satellite imagery
  • This data is public and used for community benefit

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • ❌ No email addresses or phone numbers
  • ❌ No user accounts or passwords
  • ❌ No names or personal identifiers
  • ❌ No tracking cookies or advertising IDs
  • ❌ No browsing history or device contacts
  • ❌ No background location tracking

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Display environmental data on public maps
  • Enable community reporting of pollution and environmental issues
  • Track tree planting impact and vegetation coverage
  • Generate environmental statistics for public benefit and accountability

5. Data Sharing and Public Information

All environmental reports and tree locations are PUBLIC by design. This is core to our mission of environmental accountability.

  • Reports with location and photos are visible to all users
  • Tree registrations are permanent public environmental records
  • We do not share, sell, or monetize your data with third parties
  • No third-party advertising or analytics tracking

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate GreenSpot:

  • Firebase (Google): Database and backend services - Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary: Photo storage and optimization - Privacy Policy
  • WAQI: Air quality data - Privacy Policy
  • Groq AI: Species recommendations - Privacy Policy

6. Your Rights and Control

  • All reports are anonymous by default
  • You cannot delete specific reports after submission (environmental accountability requires permanent records)
  • Tree registrations are permanent public records
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to stop future data collection

7. Data Security

  • All data transmission encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Photos stored on secure Cloudinary infrastructure
  • Firebase backend with security rules
  • No passwords or authentication tokens stored
  • Regular security audits and updates

8. Children's Privacy

GreenSpot is suitable for ages 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

GreenSpot is available globally. Environmental reports and tree registrations are public data accessible worldwide for environmental accountability. By using GreenSpot, you consent to your environmental data being publicly visible.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Legal Basis for Processing

We process data based on:

  • Your explicit consent when using the app
  • Legitimate interest in environmental protection and public accountability
  • Public interest in environmental monitoring and civic engagement

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions, concerns, or requests:

  • Email: [Create an email like: greenspot.contact@gmail.com]
  • Project: GreenSpot Environmental Monitoring Platform
  • GitHub: [Your GitHub username if applicable]

13. Compliance

GreenSpot is designed to minimize personal data collection and maximize privacy. We comply with applicable data protection laws including GDPR principles of data minimization and purpose limitation.
